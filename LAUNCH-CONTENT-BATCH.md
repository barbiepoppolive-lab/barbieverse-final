# Barbieverse — Launch Content Batch

**Purpose:** Get your social accounts looking like an active, established agency *before* outreach/comment activity starts sending people to check your profile out. If someone clicks through from a comment and finds three posts and a bio, the credibility work of that comment is undone.

**Ground rules followed:**
- Every number/fact below is pulled directly from barbieverse.org (hero stats, earnings section, FAQ, academy) — nothing invented.
- No fabricated testimonials with fake names/photos. Fake "customer" quotes are deceptive advertising, not just a style risk — the homepage's testimonial carousel already needs real creators swapped in before launch, and the same rule applies here. Post #9 below is the one "proof" post, and it uses your real, verifiable credentials (Wealth Level, Agency ID) instead of invented people.
- Tone follows your existing brand voice: empowering not preachy, authentic not polished, no corporate speak ("leverage," "unlock," "exclusive").

**How to use this doc:** 10 core ideas, each written out for Instagram / Facebook / Twitter(X) / TikTok+Moj, plus a visual brief telling you (or whoever's designing) exactly what to make or screenshot. Post in whatever order fits your calendar — Day 1 and Day 2 posts are marked as the two to get up first since they carry the most credibility weight.
